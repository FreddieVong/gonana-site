Bringing family?

Questions guests ask

Can nana host a company dinner or team night?

How many people can we bring?

WhatsApp us with your date, time and number of guests and we will hold your tables.

No deposit. Tables are held for 15 minutes.

Guests order from the food and drinks menu. WhatsApp us with your date and number of people and we will hold your tables.

Where is nana?

2 Jalan SS 13/3A, Subang Jaya Industrial Estate, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor. Search "nana SS13" on Waze or Grab.

Can under-18s come?

Under-18 guests are welcome for dinner but must leave by 8:30pm. After 8:30pm nana is strictly 18+.
