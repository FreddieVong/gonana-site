Good to know 须知

Can I bring someone under 18?

Yes, in the early evening. Under-18 guests must leave by 8:30pm; the bar is strictly 18+ after that.可以在傍晚时段来，未满 18 岁须在 8:30pm 前离开，之后严格 18+。

Can I smoke or vape?

Vapes and e-cigarettes are fine. No cigarettes inside.电子烟 / vape 可以，场内不可抽烟。

Is there a minimum spend?

Only the stage sofa: RM300 on Fri, Sat and public-holiday eves. No sofa minimum on weekdays or on the public holiday itself.只有舞台沙发有 RM300 最低消费，且仅限周五、周六和公假前夕。

Do I need to book? Is there a deposit?

Walk-ins are welcome any day. On Fri, Sat and public-holiday eves, WhatsApp ahead to +60 12-897 4221. No deposit; we hold your table 15 minutes past your time. Chat in English or Chinese.每天都欢迎 walk-in；周五、周六和公假前夕建议先 WhatsApp 订位。无需订金，座位保留 15 分钟。

When is the band on?

Every night from 9pm to 12:15am. The week's line-up goes up every Monday, here and on Instagram @nana.ss13.每晚 9pm 至 12:15am。每周一在本网站和 Instagram 公布整周阵容。

Is there a happy hour?

Yes, before 8:30pm: beer buckets (5 bottles) buy 1 free 1, 10 glasses of Guinness for RM175, crispy hand-rolls from RM6.80, and the pork chop, chargrilled chicken and chicken chop at RM16.80.8:30pm 前：啤酒桶（5 瓶）买一送一，Guinness 10 杯 RM175，手卷 RM6.80 起，扒类 RM16.80。

Can I keep an unfinished bottle?

Yes. We keep spirits for 60 days and beer for 30 days.洋酒可寄存 60 天，啤酒可寄存 30 天。

Is it halal?

No. nana is a bar, and the menu includes pork and alcohol.非清真：菜单有猪肉和酒精饮料。

Do you have drinks without alcohol?

Yes. Most signature cocktails come alcohol-free, from RM25.大部分招牌鸡尾酒都有无酒精版本，RM25 起。

Can I celebrate a birthday here?

Yes. In your birthday month the Flaming Lamborghini is RM68 instead of RM128, and 3-inch cakes are RM28 to RM29. Nana Play members also get a free birthday treat. WhatsApp ahead to hold a table.生日月火焰兰博基尼只要 RM68（原价 RM128），3 寸蛋糕 RM28–29。Nana Play 会员另有免费生日礼。

Is there a membership?

Yes, Nana Play. Every RM100 on your bill earns a chip, and chips buy fries, cocktails, even a bottle of champagne, one reward a night. Join free with a Malaysian phone number; nothing to download.账单每 RM100 赚 1 个筹码，免费加入，不用下载 App。
