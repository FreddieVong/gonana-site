Questions guests ask

What time is the live band at nana?

Live music runs every night from 9pm to 12:15am.

What are nana's opening hours?

nana is open daily from 5pm to 2am. Dinner is served from 5pm and the kitchen closes at 12:45am.

Where is nana?

2 Jalan SS 13/3A, Subang Jaya Industrial Estate, 47500 Subang Jaya, Selangor. Search "nana SS13" on Waze or Grab.

How do I book a table?

Can under-18s come?

Under-18 guests are welcome for dinner but must leave by 8:30pm. After 8:30pm nana is strictly 18+.

Is there a minimum spend?

Only the stage sofa on Friday, Saturday and public-holiday-eve nights (RM300). No minimum on weekdays.
